\caption{\textbf{The disagreement-aware Probabilistic U-Net (training).}
Outside the dashed box, the original model: the posterior sees the image and
\emph{one} randomly drawn grader $Y_g$ (green outline), and the sampled $z$
(green block, 6 channels) is concatenated to the last U-Net activation
(32 channels) and the three $1{\times}1$ convolutions of $f_{\text{comb}}$ combine
them; block width reads as channel count.
Inside the box, our extension. Two things the drawing encodes: $F(x)$ is tapped
\emph{before} $z$, so $\hat D$ depends on the image alone; and the $K$ passes
behind $U$ draw fresh $z_k$ from the \emph{prior}. \textbf{Every thumbnail is
real}, from test crop \texttt{LIDC-IDRI-0217} decoded by our trained full model
and zoomed to the nodule; $\Dgt$, $U$ and $\hat D$ share one $[0,1]$ colour
scale, so they can be compared directly. The three $Q^{(k)}$ shown are non-empty
draws --- 8 of the 16 samples for this crop are empty, though all four graders
segmented the nodule.}
